A score earns a grade when it reaches that grade's percentage floor exactly — nothing is rounded up to meet it. Percentage-of-maximum scales assume the test itself is calibrated: if a paper turns out harder than intended, the scale penalises the class rather than the paper, so it is worth glancing at the spread of results before the marks are final.
